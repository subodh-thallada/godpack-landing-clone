FAQ

Are the cards real?

Yes. Every pack contains one real Pokémon card: a Near Mint raw card or a PSA- or CGC-graded slab. Master packs hold graded slabs only. You can have the card shipped to you or sell it back to your Godpack balance.

Can I see the odds before I open a pack?

Yes. Each pack shows its lowest and highest card values and a full odds table before you buy.

How much does shipping cost?

Shipping costs $5 per card in the US and $10 per card to Canada, with one card per shipment.

How does selling a card back work?

You can sell a card back for its card value minus a sell-back fee: 5% as standard, lower for VIP members. The payout goes to your Godpack balance.

Who can use Godpack?

Godpack is for collectors aged 18 or older, or the age of majority where they live if that is higher.
